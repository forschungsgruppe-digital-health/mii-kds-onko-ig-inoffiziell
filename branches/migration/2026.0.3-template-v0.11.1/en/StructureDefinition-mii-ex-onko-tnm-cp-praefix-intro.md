The c/p/u prefix is used in the TNM classification to state the method of
classification:

- **c** (clinical): clinical classification, based on clinical findings before
  the start of therapy
- **p** (pathological): pathological classification, based on pathohistological
  examination after surgical removal
- **u** (ultrasound): classification by means of ultrasound (e.g. endoscopic
  ultrasound)

In the profiling at hand, the profiles of the T, N and M categories all use the
same extension.
