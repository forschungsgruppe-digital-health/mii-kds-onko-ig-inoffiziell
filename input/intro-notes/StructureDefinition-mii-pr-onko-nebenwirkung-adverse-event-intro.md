This profile describes the adverse effect (Nebenwirkung) of radiotherapy and systemic therapy in oncology.

The kind of adverse effect can be described either as a CTCAE term or as a MedDRA number.
Further guidance can be taken from the Dokumentationsleitfaden https://plattform65c.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/Dokumentat/pages/93683749/Nebenwirkungen
In addition, the Plattform §65c provides a curated list of translated CTCAE terms with their corresponding MedDRA numbers.
https://plattform65c.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/UMK/pages/15533115/Nebenwirkungen+CTCAE

Diverging from the oBDS, this profile also allows events of severity grades 1 and 2 to be stated explicitly. As a result it can happen that the cancer registry data does not record at all what kind of adverse effect is involved.
