This profile represents the m symbol of a TNM classification.
The m symbol indicates the presence of multiple tumours.

The explicit use of `m` codes for several tumours where the exact number is
unknown. Instead of `m`, a specific number can also be given.

The m symbol is to be distinguished from the M category for the assessment of
distant metastases.
